EF Core Database-First scaffold (run from VotingSystem.DataAccess after applying the latest SQL script):
dotnet ef dbcontext scaffold "Server=.;Database=VotingSystemDb;User Id=sa;Password=<password>;TrustServerCertificate=True;" Microsoft.EntityFrameworkCore.SqlServer -o AppDbContextModels -c AppDbContext --no-onconfiguring --force

Do not use Add-Migration or Update-Database. SQL scripts are the schema source of truth.
